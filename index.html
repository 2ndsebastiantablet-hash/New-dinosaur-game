    Exploring MEGAMALL

    Boulevards run between city-block sized wings of shops. Main boulevards are open to the skylights with bridges and escalators. Look for STAFF ONLY doors — they lead into service corridors behind the shops, back rooms, and stairwells down to the basement tunnels and parking.

    Almost everything is interactive: look at it and press E. Open doors, ride the coaster, Ferris wheel, carousel, drop tower, swings, teacups, bumper cars, water slides and chairlift. Bowl, putt, play the arcade, buy snacks and drinks, sit, swim, skate, bounce and ski.
